# Homology of the partition poset complex (checks C10, C11, C12)

`chains.py` builds, on top of `../partitions.py`, the reduced normalized chain complex of the pointed simplicial set `P(n)` of Definition 2.4: one generator per strict chain `min = λ_0 < … < λ_k = max` of partitions of `{1..n}` (the nondegenerate non-basepoint simplices), boundary `∂ = Σ (−1)^i d_i` with the faces `d_0` and `d_k` (which land in the basepoint) dropped, and `Σ_n` acting on the left by relabelling (no orientation sign, since relabelling preserves the order of a chain). It computes `H̃_i(P(n); Z)` in every degree — ranks over `Q` by exact `Fraction` elimination; ranks and torsion over `Z` from a sparse column-echelon form over `Z`, whose pivots all turn out to be `±1`, which exhibits a unimodular maximal minor of each image lattice and so proves every cokernel torsion-free (a dense Smith normal form is run as an independent second opinion for `n ≤ 5`) — together with an explicit basis of cycles of the top homology `H̃_{n−1}(P(n); Q)` (identity pattern on the free columns, so coordinates are read off; the basis is integral, hence also a `Z`-basis of the integer cycles), the matrix of every permutation on it, and the resulting character. `checks_homology_C10.py` asserts, for `n = 1, …, 6` (about 8 s in total; `python checks_homology_C10.py [nmax]`), that (a) `H̃_i(P(n); Z) = 0` for `i ≠ n−1` and `H̃_{n−1}(P(n); Z) ≅ Z^{(n−1)!}`, with no torsion in any degree, and (b) the character of `Σ_n` on `H̃_{n−1}(P(n); Q)` agrees on every conjugacy class with the character of `Lie(n) ⊗ sgn`, where `Lie(n) = Ind_{C_n}^{Σ_n}(ω)` (Klyachko) is computed independently by brute force from the induced-character formula, with an exact Möbius-function cross-check of the root-of-unity sums and the closed form `μ(m) m^{d−1} (d−1)!` on cycle type `m^d` printed alongside. Along the way it verifies `∂∂ = 0`, equivariance of `∂`, that the matrices define a left action (`M(e) = I`, `M(στ) = M(σ) M(τ)`), that traces are class functions, and that the Hopf trace formula reproduces the character from fixed-chain counts alone; for `n = 6` the permutation-indexed consistency checks run on class representatives, the generators of `Σ_6` and a random sample, while the character comparison itself covers every class. The two characters are printed side by side on class representatives, and the script ends with `ALL C10 CHECKS PASSED` or raises at the first failure. Note that `Lie(n) ⊗ sgn` differs from `Lie(n)` only for `n = 2` (on the transposition) and `n = 6` (on the classes `2^3` and `6`), so those two arities are the ones that actually test the sign twist; the expected homology (`|P(2)| = S^1`, `|P(3)| ≃ S^2 ∨ S^2`, `|P(4)| ≃ ⋁_6 S^3`, `|P(5)| ≃ ⋁_24 S^4`, `|P(6)| ≃ ⋁_120 S^5`) is reproduced with the simplex counts `C_1, …, C_{n−1}` shown in the output.

## The two operad structures on top homology (check C11)

`degree.py` computes, for every partition `α = {T_1, …, T_n}` of `{1..k}` (blocks ordered by least element, profile `(n; k_1, …, k_n)`), the maps induced on top homology by the two cocomposition maps compared in Theorem 1.3,

    c^lev_α, c^tr_α : |P(k)| → |P(n)| ∧ |P(k_1)| ∧ ⋯ ∧ |P(k_n)|,

both written in the chain-and-heights coordinates `(γ', u)` of `|P(k)|` (a nondegenerate chain and `0 ≤ u_1 ≤ … ≤ u_{k−1} ≤ 1`): `c^lev_α` is the levelled cut `κ_{F^{(1/2)}}` of Example A.50(2) = the half-cut cell `ψ_0` of Construction A.68 acting through the decomposition `Ψ` of Lemma 4.4 (Proposition A.71; the pointwise agreement of the two descriptions is `../checks_transport.py`): with `P = max{i : u_i ≤ 1/2}` the value is the basepoint unless `λ'_P = α`, and otherwise the front segment collapsed at `α` with heights `2u_1, …, 2u_P`, smashed with the restrictions of the back segment to the blocks with heights `2u_{P+1} − 1, …`, degenerate simplices normalized by `(s_j β, w) ~ (β, σ^j w)`; `c^tr_α` is Ching's cut of Definition A.31 transported through the levelling homeomorphism `Θ` (`../trees.py`, exact `Fraction` version): `Θ(γ', u)`, degrafting (Lemma A.28), bottom heights unchanged, the top piece on `T_i` rescaled by `[h(p(T_i)), 1] → [0, 1]`, `Θ^{-1}` on every piece, and the order-preserving identifications `T_i ≅ {1..k_i}`. Both maps are piecewise smooth on the top cells, so the coefficient of a target top cell `e = γ × β_1 × ⋯ × β_n` in `c_*(z)`, `z` a top cycle, is the sum over the preimages `(γ', u)` of a generic point `y ∈ e` of `z[γ'] · sign det Dc(u)`. The preimages are found exactly: on each source top cell the combinatorial output (target chains, and which source level feeds which target coordinate) is constant, the affine/rational coordinate maps are inverted by hand (`CellMap.invert`), the candidate is kept only if `0 < u_1 < … < u_{k−1} < 1`, and every preimage is re-verified by evaluating the forward map; a tie raises `NonGeneric` and the point is resampled. The orientation conventions are fixed in the module docstring: (O1) a `p`-simplex of `P(t)` is oriented by the order of its chain (as in `chains.py`), which in height coordinates is `(−1)^p` times the orientation of `(u_1, …, u_p)`, a factor that cancels between source and target since both have total dimension `k − 1`; (O2) a product cell of the smash product carries the product orientation in the order (`n`-factor, then the `k_i`-factors in block order), `|P(1)| = S^0` contributing no coordinate; (O3) `H̃_{k−1}(target) = H̃_{n−1}(P(n)) ⊗ ⨂_i H̃_{k_i−1}(P(k_i))` (Künneth), the basis element `K_a ⊗ K^1_{b_1} ⊗ ⋯` being the product cycle with the orientation (O2), whose coordinates are read off at the free product cells; (O4) `Θ` is a coordinate identification and introduces no sign; (O5) `Σ_k` relabels cells with coefficient `+1`, and reordering the smash factors contributes the Koszul sign `∏_{i<i', σ̄(i)>σ̄(i')} (−1)^{(k_i−1)(k_{i'}−1)}`. With these conventions the Jacobian of either map on a source cell is block triangular with positive diagonal blocks, so its sign is the sign of the permutation listing the source levels in target-coordinate order (the "shuffle sign"); `jacobian_sign_fd` recomputes the sign from the forward map by exact central differences, independently of that analysis.

`checks_homology_C11.py` (`python checks_homology_C11.py [kmax]`, default `kmax = 5`, 2 s; `kmax = 6` takes 38 s and covers all 203 partitions of `{1..6}`, 27400 target cells and 41962 affine pieces) computes both induced matrices for every partition `α` of `{1..k}`, `2 ≤ k ≤ kmax`, and asserts, before the comparison, that (a) on the unit profiles `(1;k)` and `(k;1,…,1)` both maps are `s · identity` for a global sign `s`, which it reports (`s = +1` in every case, both structures, all `k`); (b) both structures are `Σ_k`-equivariant, pointwise on random points of `|P(k)|` (`c_{σα}(σ·x) = (induced map)(c_α(x))`) and on homology (`M_{σα} A(σ) = T(α, σ) M_α` with the matrices of `chains.py` and the Koszul sign of (O5); all of `Σ_k` for `k ≤ 4`, generators plus a random sample for `k ≥ 5`); (c) arity 3 reproduces Appendix B exactly, in `Fraction` arithmetic: on `∇²_i = (min < {ī | i} < max)` with `(u_1, u_2) = (x, y)`, `c^tr(x, y) = (x, (y − x)/(1 − x))` for `x < y` and `c^lev(x, y) = (2x, 2y − 1)` for `x < 1/2 < y`, the basepoint off the square and on the other two strata, the worked input `(1/4, 3/4) ↦ (1/4, 2/3)` resp. `(1/2, 1/2)`, and the induced maps `H̃_2(P(3)) → Z`, `z ↦ z[∇²_i]`, read off from the two formulas; (d) the degree sums are integers independent of the generic point (3 random points per target cell; in fact every generic point has exactly one preimage under either map, for the levelled cut because exactly one shuffle of the block chains fits the order of the heights), and the image of every source cycle is certified to be a combination of the Künneth product cycles, hence a cycle of the target; (e) every shuffle sign agrees with the finite-difference Jacobian sign (all affine pieces for `k ≤ 4`, 25 per partition and structure for `k ≥ 5`). It then asserts C11, `(c^lev_α)_* = (c^tr_α)_*` as integer matrices, for every partition, prints the matrices for `k ≤ 4` (rows indexed by the Künneth basis, columns by the cycle basis of `chains.py`), and ends with `ALL C11 CHECKS PASSED` or raises at the first failure, printing both matrices at a disagreement.

## The operad on top cohomology and the Lie operad (check C12)

`checks_homology_C12.py` (`python checks_homology_C12.py [kmax]`, default `kmax = 5`, about 1 s; `kmax = 6` takes 16 s, almost all of it recomputing the C11 matrices) verifies that the maps of C11 assemble into an operad on the dual groups `O(k) := L(k)^∨ = H̃^{k−1}(P(k); Z)`, `L(k) = H̃_{k−1}(P(k); Z)` (the homology of `∂_k Id ≃ Map(|P(k)|, S)`, up to the grading shift), and identifies that operad. Conventions, fixed before any check and reported by the script: `O(k)` carries the basis dual to the cycle basis of `chains.py` and the right action `φ·σ = A(σ)^T φ` (`A(σ)` the action matrices of `chains.py`); `O(k)` sits in cohomological degree `k − 1`; tensor products are indexed by the dual Künneth basis in the order of `degree.target_basis`, with no sign in `(L(n) ⊗ ⋯)^∨ = O(n) ⊗ ⋯`; the only source of signs is the reordering of tensor factors, a factor of arity `p` moved past one of arity `q` costing the Koszul sign `(−1)^{(p−1)(q−1)}` (so factors of even arity anticommute with each other and all other pairs commute — the sign (O5) of C11, recomputed independently here); and for a partition `α = {T_1 < ⋯ < T_n}` of `{1..k}` (blocks in least-element order) the composition map is `γ_α := M_α^T : O(n) ⊗ O(k_1) ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ O(k_n) → O(k)`, the transpose of the C11 matrix (both structures are recomputed and re-asserted equal), a block of size 1 contributing `O(1) = Z·1`. The script asserts: (3) units, `γ_{(1;k)}(1 ⊗ x) = x` and `γ_{(k;1,…,1)}(x ⊗ 1 ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ 1) = x` on every basis vector; (1) equivariance, `γ_{σα}(y)·σ = γ_α(y·σ)` with `y·σ = koszul · (y_0·σ̄) ⊗ (factor at the block σ(T_i) moved to the slot of T_i and acted on by the induced permutation σ_i)`, for every partition and every `σ ∈ Σ_k` (`k ≤ 5`; generators plus a random sample for `k = 6`), 6634 identities for `k ≤ 5`, 936 of them with Koszul sign −1, and for every one of those the identity is also shown to *fail* with the sign +1, so the sign convention is forced by the data rather than merely consistent with it; (2) associativity, as coassociativity of the cocompositions along every chain `α ≤ β` of partitions of `{1..k}` (`α` refining `β`; `ρ = β/α` the induced partition of the blocks): `(⊗_j c_{α|B_j}) ∘ c_β` equals `(c_ρ ⊗ id) ∘ c_α` after the factors `(m; n_1, k_{C_1}; n_2, k_{C_2}; …)` are brought into the order `(m; n_1..n_m; k_1..k_n)` with the Koszul sign — 434 chains for `k ≤ 5` (223 with `α`, `β` proper and distinct; 51 with sign −1, all forced), 2905 for `k ≤ 6` (2088 nontrivial, 539 with sign −1); transposed, this is the associativity axiom `γ(γ(a; b_1..b_m); c_1..c_n) = ±γ(a; γ(b_1; c_{C_1}), …)` of the operad `O`. (4) The Lie operad: with `e` the generator of `O(2) = Z`, (i) `A((12)) = [[1]]` on `L(2)`, so `e·(12) = +e`: `e` is Σ_2-*invariant* (as it must be for `Lie ⊗ sgn`: the generator of `Lie(2) = sgn` becomes invariant after the twist); (ii) the three bracketings `[[x_1,x_2],x_3] = γ_{12|3}(e ⊗ e ⊗ 1) = (1, 0)`, `[[x_1,x_3],x_2] = γ_{13|2}(e ⊗ e ⊗ 1) = (0, 1)`, `[x_1,[x_2,x_3]] = γ_{1|23}(e ⊗ 1 ⊗ e) = (−1, −1)` in `O(3) = Z^2` satisfy the single primitive relation `[[x_1,x_2],x_3] + [[x_1,x_3],x_2] + [x_1,[x_2,x_3]] = 0`, all three signs +; an independent model check (`model_jacobi_patterns`) confirms that for a graded Lie algebra `g` (`gl(1|1)` as a Z-graded Lie superalgebra) the shift `V = g[1]` with the graded-symmetric degree-1 operation `e(sx, sy) = (−1)^{|x|} s[x, y]` satisfies exactly this sign pattern under the same Koszul conventions (`C` acquiring the sign `(−1)^{|v_1|}` of `1 ⊗ e` passing `v_1`) and none of the other three; (iii) the `(k−1)!` left-normed brackets `[[…[x_1, x_{s(2)}], …], x_{s(k)}]`, `s(1) = 1`, built root-first as `γ_{T | {s(k)}}(e ⊗ − ⊗ 1)`, agree with their leaf-first construction `γ_{s(1)s(2) | singletons}(z ⊗ e ⊗ 1 ⋯)` (an explicit instance of (2)) and form a Z-basis of `O(k)`: the determinants of their coordinate matrices are +1, +1, −1, +1 for `k = 2, 3, 4, 5` (and +1 for `k = 6`), by fraction-free elimination with a `Fraction` second opinion. Consequence: in arities ≤ 5 (≤ 6 with `kmax = 6`) `O` is a graded operad generated by a symmetric element `e` of cohomological degree 1 satisfying the Jacobi relation above; the free operad on such an `e` modulo that relation is the operadic suspension of `Lie` (arity `k`: `Lie(k) ⊗ sgn_k` in cohomological degree `k − 1`, i.e. `Σ^{1−k} Lie(k) ⊗ sgn_k` homologically, whose algebras `V` are the graded modules with a graded Lie algebra structure on `Σ^{−1}V`), whose arity-`k` part is free of rank `(k−1)!` on the left-normed brackets, so its map to `O` is a surjection between free Z-modules of equal rank, hence an isomorphism of operads in these arities. The script ends with `ALL C12 CHECKS PASSED` or raises at the first failure, printing both sides of the failing identity.
